# Instructions for Claude

This repository is a journalist's personal website. GitHub Pages publishes it from the main branch.

- Commit every change straight to main and push it, so the site updates. Don't open pull requests or leave work on other branches.
- Use my words exactly as I give them: my About text, headlines, outlet names and dates. Don't rewrite, shorten, summarize or add to them. If something is missing, leave a clearly marked spot and ask me.
- Never add facts about me that I didn't give you.
- Keep the site simple: one index.html with its styles inside it, and photos in an images folder. No build tools or frameworks.
- When you finish, tell me in plain words what changed, and that the live site can take a minute or two to update.

## Site style

Keep every change in this look: clean serif fonts, feminine but clean and academic. (Inspired by a magazine site Emma likes. Take the feel, not its logo, layout or branding.)

- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for the name and the line under it; EB Garamond for everything else. Both load from Google Fonts. No sans-serif fonts.
- Name: very large, regular weight (not bold), at the top left.
- Line under the name: Cormorant Garamond italic, in the muted gray.
- Section labels (About, Clips, etc.): small uppercase EB Garamond with wide letter spacing, under a thin dark rule that spans the column.
- Colors: white background, near-black text (#1c1a1a), muted gray (#6b6363), and one rose-crimson accent (#b3243c) used only for links. Keep the dark-mode colors that match.
- Links: accent color with a thin underline, no default blue.
- Layout: one narrow centered column (about 680px), lots of white space, no boxes, shadows, rounded corners or decoration.
- Headshot: images/headshot.jpg, shown as a circle to the left of the About text (above it on phones). The circle is the one exception to "no rounded corners".
- Clips: a list, newest first. Each clip has a small uppercase muted line with the outlet and date, then the headline in Cormorant Garamond, linked to the story (opens in a new tab) in near-black (turns accent color on hover). Thin pale line between clips. Write clip dates as Month Day, Year (for example "November 11, 2026"); Emma chose this style. Use the add-clip skill to add one.
- Social: an X logo (links to https://x.com/emmaeruby) sits under the contact email, in the accent color, with no underline.
- Rosie: Emma's dog, as a small hand-drawn illustration (images/rosie.svg, with a cream sticker outline so she shows in light and dark mode) fixed in the bottom-right corner. Clicking her pops up a small hand-sketched speech bubble (loose pencil-style outline) with a sketched red heart (soft red wash, pencil hatching). The bubble is the other exception to "no rounded corners or decoration".
- Browser tab icon: the initials "ER" in capitals, Cormorant Garamond, cream letters on an accent-color square (images/favicon.svg, with PNG copies favicon-32.png and apple-touch-icon.png).
- Placeholders for missing content: pale blush box with a dashed border and italic text.
- Keep the page working on phones.
